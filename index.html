About

Building Digital Products That Actually Ship

I'm Brian Murithi, a full-stack developer and the founder of DigiWebs. I build practical, secure, and scalable web and mobile products — from payment-integrated booking platforms to business dashboards and records systems — for founders, SMEs, and organisations who need software they can rely on.

I combine technical depth with a business-first mindset: every system I build is judged by whether it solves a real problem, not by how impressive the stack looks. That means clean architecture, security handled from day one, and interfaces people actually enjoy using.

I work with clients remotely, wherever they're based — bringing the same standard of engineering to a five-person startup as I would to an enterprise team.
